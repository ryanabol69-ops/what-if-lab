Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who this covers

The What If Lab app is a private tool used by the owner of the What If Lab accounts. This policy explains what the app reads from TikTok and what it does with it. The app does not collect information from people who watch the videos, and it has no other users.

What the app can access

When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok asks them to approve these permissions, and the app gets nothing beyond them:

  • Basic profile information of the connected account: its open ID, display name and profile picture, and its profile link, bio and whether it is verified.
  • The connected account’s public videos and its totals, such as follower, like and video counts.
  • The ability to upload videos to the connected account, as a draft or as a direct post.

How it is used

This information is used only to publish What If Lab’s own videos to its own account, and to read how those videos are doing (views, likes and comments) so the owner can make better ones. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is kept

The permission tokens that TikTok issues are held by the integration service the owner uses to connect their accounts (Composio) and on the owner’s own computer. Video numbers are kept on the owner’s computer. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Keeping and removing data

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in their TikTok account’s permissions settings, or in the integration service, which ends the app’s access. The owner can then delete what is stored on their computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from children or from viewers.

Changes

This policy may change. The date above shows the latest version.
