What If Lab

What If Lab makes short, original science videos that answer big “what if?” questions in about 30 seconds. You can find them on YouTube (@TheLabOfWhatIf) and Instagram (@thelabofwhatif), and on TikTok.

This site belongs to the What If Lab app, a private tool that the owner of the What If Lab accounts uses to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It is not open to the public and has no sign-up.
